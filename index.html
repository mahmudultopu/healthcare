FAQ

Questions?
We're Here.

Can't find what you need? Our care team replies within an hour.

care@newme.health
How do online consultations work?

Book a "Video call" visit, then join from the web app at your appointment time. No downloads needed. It works in any modern browser on phone or computer.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes. We work with more than 60 insurance providers. Add your policy in the web app and we'll show what's covered before you book.

How quickly will I get my lab results?

Most routine results are ready within 24 hours and appear in the Records section of your web app, with a doctor's note explaining them.

Can I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Yes, free of charge up to 2 hours before your visit. Open "Appointments" in the web app and choose cancel or reschedule.

Is my medical data secure?

Your records are encrypted in transit and at rest, and only you decide which doctors can see them.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call your local emergency number or our 24/7 line at +1 (800) 555-0199 right away. Do not wait for an online appointment for chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke or severe bleeding.
